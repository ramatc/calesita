## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui, Lucide React, localStorage. No backend, database, or authentication in the MVP.

## Users

Groups of friends who are physically together and do not know what to do next. One person picks up a phone or opens a shared computer. The target scene is a gathering of about eight people.

## Product Purpose

Calesita hosts party games that happen in real life. The web app acts as host, organizer, and moderator: it shows prompts, explains rules, generates challenges, runs turns and timers, assigns secret roles, and keeps score. Success means a group goes from "we are bored" to playing in very few steps.

## Positioning

Not a video game platform and not an online multiplayer service. One shared device moderates a game played face to face, with no download and no registration.

## Operating Context

Used at night at a gathering: dim rooms, noise, one device passed from hand to hand, held at arm's length or left on a table for the group to read. Secret information must survive the device changing hands.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Fifteen catalog games in three categories; five are playable in the MVP, the rest show "Próximamente" with no misleading start button.
- UI copy is Rioplatense Spanish.
- No real-time multiplayer, accounts, or admin panels in the MVP.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Calesita. Slogan: "La juntada sigue girando."
- Dark by default, violet ground and primary color, accents in orange, yellow, pink, and turquoise. Large cards, rounded edges, modern type, subtle motion, and illustrations tied to each game.
- Must not look like an enterprise dashboard or a generic SaaS template.

## Evidence on Hand

No testimonials, usage data, or press exist. Do not invent any.

## Product Principles

- Play fast: open the site and start in very few steps.
- One device: never require everyone to have a phone.
- Zero confusion: the next step is always obvious.
- Clear rules: short explanations, with examples when needed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Semantic buttons, keyboard navigation, labels, and adequate contrast. Challenges must never be dangerous, humiliating, or mandatory, and any challenge can be skipped.
