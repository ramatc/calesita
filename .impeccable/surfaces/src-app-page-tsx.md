## Direction contract

THESIS: A neighbourhood calesita at night, not a game launcher. Refuses the SaaS card grid and the neon-on-black party template.
OWN-WORLD: Saturated violet night ground; canopy stripes in pink, yellow, turquoise and orange; marquee bulbs; Shrikhand for the marquee voice, Bricolage Grotesque for everything read in play. Tickets and the sortija ring are the component language.
STORY: The group sees the carousel, trusts it is a game, picks one, and plays.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Marquee wordmark and slogan beside the lit carousel; on mobile they stack, wordmark first.
SIGNATURE: Sorprendeme is catching the sortija.
RISK: Illustration is hand-drawn SVG; per-game illustrations must match its flat, chunky grammar.
